Names and messages are processed entirely in your browser and your downloads are created on your own device. Nothing you type is sent to a server — and there is no account or password on this page for anyone to steal.

Your first download each visit asks for a quick human check — a tiny sum only a person bothers to solve. It stops bots and scripts from hammering the downloads, and downloads are also rate-limited.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about making, downloading and sharing your wish cards.

Is the Wishes Card Generator free?

Yes — completely free. There is no sign-up, no account and nothing to install. Type a name, choose an occasion, unlock a message and download your card, all in your browser.

How do I download my card as a picture?

Type the person's name, pick an occasion, tap a sealed message to unlock it, choose one of the 18 designs, then press Download picture. You get a high-quality PNG image that is ready to share on WhatsApp, Facebook or anywhere else. The first download of each visit asks for a quick human check to keep bots away.

What is the 3D gift download, and how does the recipient unlock it?

Download 3D gift saves a small interactive gift file instead of a picture. Send that file to the person — when they open it, they see a sealed, floating 3D gift box with their name on it, and your message stays hidden. They tap the gift, the lid flies off with a confetti burst, and your card flips open in 3D. They can even lock it again and replay the surprise.

Can I get wishes in Roman Urdu?

Yes. Use the Message language switch in Step 1 to choose Roman Urdu — messages are then written in easy-to-read Roman Urdu (Urdu in Latin letters), perfect for WhatsApp. English is available too, in Heartfelt, Joyful, Funny and Formal styles.

Are the messages really different every time?

The generator builds each set of six messages from a very large pool of openings, middles and endings for every occasion and style, and never repeats a line inside one set. That makes the exact same set coming up twice extremely unlikely — virtually never.

How many ready-made messages are there, and how do they auto-select?

There are 160+ ready-made messages in the library, plus the fresh generator. Just pick an occasion — Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, Eid and the rest — and that occasion's messages are automatically loaded as sealed cards in Step 2. Tap Fresh new messages to rotate to a different set from the same occasion's library.

Which occasions are covered?

Thirteen occasions: Birthday, Wedding, Anniversary, New Baby, Congratulations, Graduation, Eid Mubarak, Diwali, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day, Thank You and Good Luck — each with its own messages, headline and decoration style. See wishes for every occasion above.

Is my name or message sent anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your own device: names and messages are processed in your browser and your picture and 3D gift downloads are created locally. Nothing you type is sent to a server, and there is no account or password on this page.
